## Overview & Scope

MyTaskOwl is a comprehensive workforce and business management SaaS. I worked across the full stack — building features on the Angular web client, and designing the database architecture and backend logic from the ground up. I set up the Laravel backend and Eloquent database system with a focus on clean, understandable patterns so my coworkers could easily step in and contribute as business data rapidly grew.

## Core Features Built & Technical Highlights

- **Dynamic Payroll Rule Builder:** Engineered a flexible rule-engine UI and backend parser allowing business owners and HR managers to construct custom payroll formulas, deductions, and calculation logic tailored to their company policies.
- **Activity & Time Tracking System:** Integrated with a low-level .NET runtime engine to accurately capture and log employee application activity, work hours, and productivity metrics.

![Time Tracking Dashboard](/web-time-tracking.png)

- **Complex Inventory Management:** Designed the database schema and business logic for multi-branch stock movements, reorder thresholds, and inventory tracking.
- **High-Volume Analytics Dashboard:** Built reporting dashboards aggregating millions of records. As data scaled, I continuously profiled and optimized database indexes and queries to keep page loads fast and responsive.
- **Cross-Platform Realtime Sync:** Connected WebSockets and push notifications to deliver instant updates, task alerts, and chat across both web and mobile users.

## Engineering Trade-offs & Solutions

- **Rule Engine Flexibility vs. Performance:** Instead of hardcoding payroll rules into rigid database columns, built an interpretable rule structure that evaluates dynamically while keeping calculation batches performant for large employee rosters.
- **Data Growth vs. Dashboard Responsiveness:** When summary queries slowed down under heavy historical data, introduced composite covering indexes and indexed summary aggregates to avoid expensive full-table scans.
- **Layered Code Architecture:** Kept business logic isolated in dedicated service layers and repository patterns so other team members could easily understand, test, and contribute without breaking existing workflows.

## Impact

Delivered a reliable, multi-featured SaaS platform that handles mission-critical time tracking, payroll computations, and inventory for businesses daily.
